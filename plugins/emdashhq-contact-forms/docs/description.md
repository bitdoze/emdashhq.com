Build a contact form in the admin, drop it on any page with a block, and read every submission in the built-in inbox. Notification email goes through the provider your site already uses — the plugin never talks to an email service itself.

## What you get

- **Forms** — create and edit forms, manage fields (text, email, tel, url, number, textarea, select, checkbox), reorder them, enable or disable a form without deleting it.
- **Submissions inbox** — every entry stored in your site's database with the page it came from; filter by form, status, or email result; mark read or archive; resend the notification; copy as CSV.
- **Email log** — every send attempt with status, duration, and the provider's error message, so a delivery failure is diagnosable instead of silent.
- **Dashboard widget** — unread count, active forms, and the most recent submissions.
- **Anti-spam** — hidden honeypot, minimum-fill-time trap, and a per-IP hourly limit on by default; optional Cloudflare Turnstile when passive traps are not enough.
- **Retention** — optionally delete submissions and log entries older than a number of days.

Your visitor's address never appears in the public form definition, and a form's recipient is only ever in the admin.
